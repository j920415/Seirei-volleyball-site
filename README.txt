Seirei High School website update

This update keeps multi-day event bars within the currently displayed month.
For example, Golden Week appears through April 30 on the April calendar, then
starts again on May 1 in the May calendar and continues through May 6.
Adjacent-month date cells do not show event bars.

Replace index.html, style.css, and script.js in your existing site folder.
Keep your existing images/ and audio/ folders.
